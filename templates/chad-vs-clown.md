# Chad vs 🤡 Templates — Copy-Paste Ready

All examples verbatim from @BowTiedSalesGuy's public threads. Replace bracketed placeholders. Keep the lower-case and punctuation quirks — they are load-bearing (high-status casualness).

## Template 1 — The 1-1-1 pattern interrupt (Jan 2022)

Subject: `[Chad's company] x [Prospect first name only]`

> Hi [First name],
>
> We just helped a company in your space (unfortunately can't say which one) reduce their OPEX by $977k.
>
> perhaps we should explore some opportunities

Why it works: status gap (company vs individual), emotional trigger ("in your space" = threat/curiosity), intrigue (unnamed client), lower-case "perhaps" = IDGAF high status, "some opportunities" invites the inner opportunist without overselling. One idea, one implied action (reply), seconds to read.

## Template 2 — Begin with the End, supporting pains (Apr 2022)

Subject: `[your company name]`

> [First name], can you take a look here and let me know if we should jump on a call?
>
> With [trigger event] we're supporting many [role] execs like yourself who are struggling with:
>
> -[emotional pain 1]
>
> -[emotional pain 2]
>
> look forward to hearing back -

Why it works: direct visual ask ("take a look here"), "supporting" not "helping" (partnership, not rescue), dash-prefixed pains = emotional not features, trailing "-" = cliffhanger.

## Template 3 — Condensed (fluff-cut) version of a company template

Subject: `[prospect's company name]`

> Hi [First name],
>
> congrats on the [trigger event, e.g. Series A round].
>
> In my experience, [company stage] have their attention on [observed priority].
>
> We've been providing unique solutions for companies like [competitor 1], [competitor 2], and [competitor 3]
>
> I don't suppose you'd have a few min to jump on a call and see if we can do the same for you?

"I don't suppose" = presumptuous-but-soft high status. Note the cut version omits every adjective the 🤡 version had ("seamlessly," "top candidates," "very impressive").

## Subject line bank (🐲)

| Situation | Subject |
|---|---|
| Default | `[Prospect's company name]` |
| Partnership feel | `[Your name] x [First name]` |
| Pure intrigue | `an idea` / `?` / `relevant?` |
| Slight confusion hook | `a bit confused` |
| Binary reply | `quick yes/no` |
| Referral lever | `via [high-status referral name]` |
| Competitor FOMO | `Why arent you benefitting like [competitor]?` |
| Follow-up bump | `[First name]?` |

**Banned 🤡 subjects:** "Quick question" · "mind taking a quick look?" · "Just following up" · "quick intro" · anything ChatGPT-polished.

## Follow-up cadence (max 3 touches)

1. **Day 0** — one of the templates above.
2. **Day 2** — reply to your own thread: subject/body = `[First name]?`
3. **Day 4–5** — one nudge only, e.g. the Kermit meme, or:
   > [First name] - did you still want to meet?

**Ghosted after a meeting (sent once, any time later):**
> you disappeared

Or the nuclear version: subject line `you disappeared`, body empty.

**No-show on booked call:**
> Hi [First name], I was waiting for you in the Zoom but decided to jump into another meeting. If you're still interested in meeting you can find a new time to book here [link]. If I don't hear back from you I will assume that you've found a vendor and will close out your file.

## LinkedIn DM (🐲 shape)

> If you guys are seeing [specific observed problem] and want a second opinion LMK

Rules: never mention your company name; direct & concise; tie to a recent post of theirs with positive emotional context if one exists. Skip generic connection notes entirely — a bad note is worse than none.
